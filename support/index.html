SeizLog is a private seizure diary for people with epilepsy and their caregivers. There is no account: your diary stays on your iPhone.

SeizLog is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. Always talk to your doctor. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device, in the app's local database. The developer never receives it. Like all app data, it may be included in your iPhone backup (iCloud or computer) depending on your iOS settings.

How do I log a seizure that already happened?

Tap the “+” button, then “Log a past seizure instead”. You can pick the real date and time.

How do I edit or delete an entry?

Open the Journal tab, tap the entry, then tap Edit. Delete is at the bottom of the entry.

How do I share my diary with my doctor?

In the Trends tab, tap the export icon at the top right. Choose a PDF summary or a CSV file (for a spreadsheet), then send it with the iOS share sheet.

How do I back up or move my diary to a new iPhone?

Home › Settings (gear icon) › Back up: save the file where you like (Files, AirDrop, e-mail). On the new iPhone, install SeizLog, then Settings › Restore and choose the file. SeizLog also keeps a daily copy in Files › On My iPhone › SeizLog.

Medication reminders don't appear

Check iOS Settings › Notifications › SeizLog and make sure notifications are allowed. Reminder times can be changed in the Treatment tab.

How do I change the language?

SeizLog follows your iPhone language. You can also choose one in Settings › Language.

What seizure types does SeizLog use?

The ILAE 2017 operational classification: focal, generalized and unknown onset. Sources are listed in Settings › Sources.

Is SeizLog available on Android?

Not yet.

I found a bug

E-mail contact@just4work.com with a short description, your device model and iOS version. Screenshots help.
