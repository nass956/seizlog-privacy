English

In short: SeizLog has no account and the developer never receives your diary.

What you enter

Seizures, medications, doses, notes and settings are stored only on your device, in the app's local database. The developer has no access to them. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. SeizLog does not use Apple Health.

Network use

When it starts, the app reads a public file (https://nass956.github.io/seizlog-privacy/version.json) to check whether a newer version is available. No personal or health data is sent. As with any web request, the hosting provider (GitHub) receives technical connection data such as the IP address.

Notifications

Medication reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. No server is involved. They do not show medication names on the lock screen.

Exports and backups

PDF, CSV and backup files are created only when you ask for them. You choose where they go through the iOS share sheet; once shared, they are no longer controlled by the app. SeizLog also keeps a daily copy of your diary on your device, visible in Files › On My iPhone › SeizLog. Like all app data, your diary may be included in your device backup (iCloud or computer) depending on your iOS settings.

Deleting your data

You can delete any entry in the app. Deleting the app erases its database and the daily copy on the device, but not files you have exported elsewhere.

Children

SeizLog does not knowingly collect data from anyone; parents may keep a diary for their child on their own device.

Medical disclaimer. SeizLog is a personal diary, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, detect, predict or treat seizures and does not give medical advice.

Changes

We will update this page if anything changes and show the date above.
