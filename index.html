Privacy Policy – SeizLog

Last updated: September 10, 2026

SeizLog ("the App") is a personal seizure diary designed to help individuals track their epilepsy. Your privacy is our highest priority.

1. Data Storage

All data you enter in SeizLog — seizures, medications, notes, and settings — is stored exclusively on your device using local SQLite storage. No personal health data is ever transmitted to our servers or shared with any third party.

2. Data We Collect

SeizLog itself does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

For automatic app updates, we use Expo's update service (expo.dev). This service may collect limited technical information such as app version, device platform, and anonymous crash diagnostics to ensure the app functions correctly. This data is not linked to your identity. See Expo's Privacy Policy.

3. Push Notifications

If you enable notifications for medication reminders, these are processed entirely on your device using Apple's notification system. No notification content is transmitted to our servers.

4. Health Data

SeizLog does not integrate with Apple Health and does not share your health data with any third party. All health information remains private on your device.

5. Children's Privacy

SeizLog does not knowingly collect any data from users under the age of 13.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy occasionally. Continued use of the app following any changes constitutes your acceptance of the new terms.

7. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at: contact@just4work.fr
