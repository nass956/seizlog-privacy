About SeizLog

SeizLog is a personal seizure diary that helps people with epilepsy log and track their seizures. All data is stored locally on your device — no account, no cloud, no data sharing.

⚕️ SeizLog is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice. Always consult your healthcare provider.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is my data stored?

All your seizure and medication data is stored exclusively on your device using a local SQLite database. We have no access to your data. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all data.

Can I export my seizure logs?

Export is planned for a future version. Currently, data can be shown directly in the app to share with your neurologist.

The app won't open / crashes at launch

Try force-closing the app and reopening it. If the issue persists, try restarting your device. If it still fails, please email us at support@seizlog.app with your device model and iOS version.

How do I delete a seizure entry?

Go to the History tab, tap on the seizure entry, then tap the delete button on the detail screen.

Is SeizLog available on Android?

Android support is planned for a future release.

What seizure types does SeizLog support?

SeizLog uses the ILAE 2017 seizure classification, covering focal, generalized, and unknown onset seizures, with sub-types for each category.

I found a bug. How do I report it?

Please email support@seizlog.app with a description of the issue, your device model, and iOS version. Screenshots are welcome.
